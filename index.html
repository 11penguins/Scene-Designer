Design intelligence

Strong design intelligence rests on two closely related capabilities: scene design and scene critique.

01

Scene design

Scene design concerns constructing physically valid, functionally correct, and layout-coherent scenes while efficiently correcting physical errors and semantic and layout errors.

The gap

General models can identify errors, yet still struggle to repair and rank scenes reliably.

In 100 GPT-5.2 construction trajectories, even straightforward errors often trigger long correction chains. On Scene-OTC, pairwise judgments are easier, while coherent four-scene ranking exposes a larger deficit in domain-specific critique experience.

Framework

Learning from complementary design and critique experience.

Scene-Designer couples trajectory-level construction supervision with controlled scene preferences, then uses a frozen Critic to guide policy optimization.

01Observation · Thought · Action

Scene-OTA-35K

1K scenes and 35K construction steps retain intermediate observations, action reasoning, execution feedback, and successful recovery experience.

  • Causal trajectory consolidation
  • Error-aware candidate identification
  • Correction-effect evaluation
02Observation · Thought · Critic

Scene-OTC-15K

Controlled degradations teach the Critic to rank physical validity, functional correctness, and layout coherence at multiple severity levels.

  • Accumulating perturbations
  • Different perturbation strengths
  • Same perturbation, different objects
03Critic-guided optimization

Scene-GRPO

Multiple candidate edits branch from the same state. A frozen Critic ranks branches and compares parent-child quality to direct learning.

  • Local branch ranking
  • Parent-child improvement reward
  • Global efficiency and completeness
Scene-GRPO

Explore several edits. Continue with the strongest one.

At each editing step, the policy expands candidate actions from a shared parent scene. The Critic supplies local preferences while global rewards encourage efficient, complete construction.

Reliable scene critique

The Critic maintains a coherent preference over multiple scene degradations.

Joint Scene-OTA + Scene-OTC training improves both pairwise selection and full four-scene ranking. Five shuffled image-label orders confirm that performance does not depend on fixed positions.

Design and critique are not separate endpoints. Learning why a scene is better can help an agent decide what to build next.

Scene-Designer provides evidence that construction experience and critique experience can reinforce one another, both during training and through test-time Critic guidance.
